My Favourite Food: Stuffed Grape Leaves (Sarma)

My favourite food is stuffed grape leaves, also known as sarma. It is a traditional dish made with grape leaves filled with rice, ground beef, fresh herbs, and spices.

Ingredients:
- Grape leaves
- 1 1/2 cups short-grain rice
- 1 large onion
- 12 oz ground beef
- Olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tsp allspice
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- Fresh parsley
- Fresh dill
- Fresh mint
- 1-2 tomatoes
- Chicken broth or water
- Juice of 2 lemons

Instructions:
1. Rinse the grape leaves and let them drain.
2. Soak the rice in water for about 15-20 minutes and drain it.
3. Cook the chopped onion with olive oil, then add the ground beef and cook until browned.
4. Season the meat with salt, pepper, allspice, and cumin.
5. Mix the cooked meat with the rice and fresh herbs.
6. Place a small amount of filling in each grape leaf, fold the sides, and roll it carefully.
7. Arrange the stuffed grape leaves in a pot.
8. Add chicken broth or water and cook over medium heat for about 30 minutes.
9. Add lemon juice and continue cooking on low heat for another 30-45 minutes.
10. Let the dolma rest for about 20 minutes before serving.

I like to serve stuffed grape leaves with yogurt and lemon.
