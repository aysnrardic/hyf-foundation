My Second Favourite Food: Lahmacun

My second favourite food is lahmacun. It is made with a very thin dough topped with a mixture of minced meat, vegetables, herbs, and spices.

Ingredients:
- Dough
- 1/2 sweet red pepper
- 1 shallot
- 2 garlic cloves
- Fresh parsley
- 7 oz ground lamb or ground beef
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground allspice
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp Aleppo-style pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 4 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Lemon wedges

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. Add the red pepper, shallot, garlic, and parsley to a food processor and chop them.
3. Add the ground lamb or beef, spices, salt, tomato paste, and olive oil. Mix until well combined.
4. Divide the dough into four equal pieces.
5. Roll each piece of dough very thinly.
6. Spread the meat mixture evenly over the dough, almost to the edges.
7. Bake for about 5-7 minutes, until the dough and meat are fully cooked.
8. Repeat with the remaining dough and topping.
9. Squeeze some fresh lemon juice over the lahmacun before serving.

I like to eat lahmacun with fresh lemon, parsley, and onion.
